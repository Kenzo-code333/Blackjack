Power-ups (drive through ❓ boxes)

  • 🚀 Speed Boost — a burst of nitro.
  • 🚀×3 Triple Boost — three short boosts, one per press.
  • 🟤 Mud Splash — throws mud at the racer ahead: a big slowdown for 2 seconds and mud on their screen!
  • 🎯 Homing Rocket — chases the racer ahead and flips them into a spin.
  • ⚡ Lightning — stuns every racer ahead of you for a moment.
  • 💥 Shockwave — blasts nearby cars away from you.
  • 👻 Ghost — 5 seconds of driving through cars, immune to every attack.
  • 🌀 Warp Jump — teleports you 45 m up the road.
  • 🛡️ Shield — blocks one attack.
  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — pulls nearby coins to you.
  • 🛢️ Oil Slick — drop it behind you to spin out chasers.

Blue arrow pads give a free boost, and yellow ramps launch you into the air. Collect 🪙 coins on the road, then spend them in the garage. Win a race to earn a 🎁 treasure chest with coins, parts or exclusive upgrades!
